01

From published notice to appraisal: the expropriation workflow

Expropriation · weekly cycle
  1. 1
    Search

    Every week, region by region, through seven channels: municipal notice boards, resolutions, local press, operators, tenders, planning amendments and indications of possession.

  2. 2
    Evidence

    Every finding includes a source URL, an exact excerpt and a hash to verify its integrity. Without evidence, it is rejected.

  3. 3
    Compare

    Finding the same notice twice is a duplicate. A new notice about the same project is a new finding.

  4. 4
    Deliver

    A list ready for the professionals handling the cases, recorded only after it has been read back at its destination.

  5. 5
    Appraise prototype

    Case documents are reviewed against appraisal rules. The output is a proposed assessment, with sources cited for every item.

  6. 6
    Validate

    A lawyer and a technical professional review the assessment. Only then does it become an appraisal.

The problem

Notices initiating expropriation are scattered across notice boards, resolutions and local press. Tracking them manually, project by project, does not scale.

What I built

A workflow where AI agents search and read according to written rules, while professionals make the decisions. Priority goes to taking possession and urgent occupation.

The result

Each cycle delivers a verified list of projects and notices, with sources and dates, without owners' personal data.

02

Finding the right land for a plant

Renewables · in production · live demo ↗
The problem

Assessing a site's suitability means combining cadastre, power grid, solar irradiation and constraints. Doing it manually means unwieldy spreadsheets.

What I built

A 3D platform that compares sites and gives each a score from 0 to 100, with cadastral mapping and distances to primary substations.

The result

Over 21,900 georeferenced parcels and 116 assessed lots, totalling 709.7 hectares.

03

Knowing who remains exposed to motorway noise

Major infrastructure · detailed design data
The problem

Along sections B2 and C, the task was to identify buildings still exceeding noise limits even with barriers in place.

What I built

A 3D mapping console with every receptor at its documented location, the proposed route, interchanges and buildings.

The result

6,910 receptors surveyed, with 39 still exceeding limits after noise barriers.

Sheet 03 · Method

From problem to tool.

  1. 01

    The real problem

    I start with the work of the people who will use the tool: available data, constraints and the result they actually need.

  2. 02

    A working prototype

    Often, within a few days there is something to try, including on a phone, connected to existing workflows.

  3. 03

    Human oversight

    AI agents handle the repetitive work. You keep the decisions.

The rules agents follow

  • Every data point has a source, a date and an excerpt you can check.
  • An indication remains an indication: without the official document, it does not become a certainty.
  • A delivery is complete only after it has been read back at its destination.
  • Personal data stays out: the records have no fields for owners' names.
  • No message is sent without an explicit instruction.
