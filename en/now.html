Now

A snapshot of what I am currently working on and thinking about (last updated: 16 November 2026). Inspired by the /now movement.

Geo-intelligence for renewables

Building specialized web crawlers and spatial analysis pipelines to scout viable land for utility-scale solar and agrivoltaic projects. I cross-reference cadastral records, distances to primary substations, and zoning restrictions to produce weighted suitability scores instead of endless raw spreadsheets.

Drone surveys and photogrammetry

A survey service for infrastructure, construction sites and the environment, from flight planning to orthophotos, point clouds, 3D models and sections. Deliverables are agreed for each assignment.

Explore the drone survey service →

Operational tools and AI agents

Engineering targeted tools that solve concrete friction in daily operations: automated document ingestion, structured quote generation, and task agents backed by deterministic logic and human review. The focus is on fast, lightweight software delivered straight to mobile.

Expropriation and technical appraisals

Coordinating technical expropriation cases and drafting formal appraisal reports (under Italian law D.P.R. 327/2001) for private owners affected by major infrastructure corridors. Organising project documents and developing valuation calculations with traceable assumptions, sources and steps.

Visual identity and clear interfaces

Designing high-contrast visual systems, vector branding, and lightweight web layouts. Clear typography, lightweight pages and readable information structures help people use complex tools.

Research and current technologies

Exploring persistent memory architectures for autonomous agents, EU AI Act compliance workflows for organizations, and headless virtualized environments on macOS and Unix.
