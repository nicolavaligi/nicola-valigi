Skills

I bring reasoning and intuition, and artificial intelligence amplifies them. I pick up the ideas, develop them by directing an agent, and deliver them as tools someone actually uses, every day.

New applications feel familiar straight away, and I enjoy studying them.

I spent twelve years inside the documents of a large infrastructure contract. There I learned to recognise the real bottleneck, which is usually not the one that gets reported. It always closes with something published, and with a measure that decides the next cycle.

AI moves fast, and it also amplifies the void. The value is in knowing what to ask it and what to refuse.

Data

I take apart public portals that have no API. I find the address that returns the real data, I write parsers that hold if a column changes, and I pull the text out of PDFs.

The archive sits in SQLite or in Postgres. What lands on the table is the signal, not the whole list.

Release

From prototype to production: REST APIs in FastAPI, serverless functions on Netlify and Supabase, on only when they are needed, keys always on the server and never in the browser. The proxies, the door in front of the service, accept only authorised domains and cap the number of requests. Access is separate for each client.

The public site is generated from the data, so the front and the archive do not diverge. Every function works from an interface, from the command line, and over an API.
