What I Do

I connect complex operational problems, technical documentation, and official datasets with automated execution. I direct AI agents and write deterministic code to turn manual, fragmented workflows into working software—often delivered in days, straight to mobile.

Whether parsing inbound requests, generating programmatic quotes, or verifying cadastral records, human oversight remains central: nothing ships without explicit sign-off. I work independently, adopt the best tool for the problem, and deliver tools teams actually rely on daily.

Data & Geospatial Intelligence

I source, clean, and georeference mission-critical geospatial data from official registries. Instead of dumping raw spreadsheets, I aggregate datasets into actionable suitability scores (0–100) to de-risk investment and land acquisition:

  • 21,900+ cadastral parcels analyzed, mapped, and attributed
  • 116 utility-scale solar parcels screened: 709.7 ha, 591.4 MWp total potential
  • 60 macro-hubs evaluated: 1,166.2 ha, 971.8 MWp pipeline
  • 2,107 high-voltage primary substations geolocated for grid interconnection
  • 277 logistics and industrial yards assessed (54.3 MWp) across 7,904 Italian municipalities

Spatial Analysis & Decision Mapping

Grid capacity, distance to overhead lines, solar irradiation, agricultural constraints, and zoning boundaries: open countryside and peri-urban buffers carry fundamentally different risks. I condense full days of desk analysis and registry cross-referencing into interactive, decision-ready GIS layers.

Interface, Visual Identity & Communication

Data loses value if the interface is hostile or generic. I design clean, high-contrast visual systems and lightweight web interfaces built for immediate comprehension. No bloated UI frameworks, generic corporate templates, or sloppy synthetic layouts.

From vector branding and operational dashboards to responsive layouts and direct copywriting: I craft visual identities and web tools with deliberate typography, sharp hierarchies, and zero decorative noise. Tools should look intentional, human, and immediately usable.

Domain Expertise: Infrastructure & Expropriation Law

Twelve years immersed in the technical, legal, and operational documentation of major infrastructure contracts taught me one rule: the real bottleneck is rarely the one flagged in status meetings.

I coordinate expropriation procedures and draft technical valuation appraisals (perizie di stima) under Italian expropriation law (D.P.R. 327/2001), protecting property owners and streamlining contentious files.

Systems & Stack

Pragmatic, terminal-first execution. Deep Unix/Linux background, running isolated environments and headless microservices via OrbStack and virtualized architectures on macOS. I build with Python (GIS/scraping), PostGIS, modern AI APIs, and lightweight web delivery.
