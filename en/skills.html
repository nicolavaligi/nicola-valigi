Construction sites, infrastructure and digital delivery

My technical approach is rooted in over a decade spent on construction sites and in project offices for major infrastructure projects during the Impregilo era (now Webuild).

Years before the rise of generative AI, I architected and built an internal platform to rigorously track subcontractors, cost accounting, and work progress statements (SAL). Managing complex physical job sites, coordinating labor, and eliminating administrative bottlenecks taught me that the real operational hurdle rarely matches what gets presented in executive boardroom decks.

That on-the-ground experience also informed my practice in expropriation proceedings (D.P.R. 327/2001): coordinating complex legal-technical files, analyzing right-of-way buffer zones, and drafting technical appraisals (perizie di stima) to safeguard property owners and unblock indemnity settlements.

Web scraping, data pipelines and PDF extraction

The most critical B2B intelligence is frequently locked inside legacy government portals, scanned PDF filings, or unindexed public registries. I build automated Python ingestion pipelines with resilient architectures:

  • Institutional & PNRR Procurement Crawlers: Continuous crawlers monitoring municipal boards, tenders, contract IDs (CIG), and public bids with automated deduplication and schema normalization
  • PDF to Supabase / SQL Pipelines: Parsing unstructured technical specs, deeds, and decrees into clean tabular formats and relational SQL stores
  • Incremental Cursors & Delta Streams: High-reliability delta architectures (since_seq) serving clean real-time feeds to consumer APIs and dashboards

Geo-intelligence: 3D GIS and cadastral data

I source, clean, and georeference mission-critical geospatial data from official registries. Instead of dumping raw spreadsheets, I aggregate datasets into actionable suitability scores (0–100) to make criteria, constraints and checks explicit when assessing investments and land acquisitions:

  • 21,900+ cadastral parcels analyzed, mapped, and attributed
  • 116 utility-scale solar parcels screened: 709.7 ha, 591.4 MWp total potential
  • 60 macro-hubs evaluated: 1,166.2 ha, 971.8 MWp pipeline
  • 2,107 high-voltage primary substations geolocated for grid interconnection
  • 277 logistics and industrial yards assessed (54.3 MWp) across 7,904 Italian municipalities
